The Human Gate & Truth Provenance

JobOS solves the two greatest risks of autonomous AI: unwanted submissions and invented resume claims.

1. Three-Tier Provenance Taxonomy

100% Provenance

USER_VERIFIED: Ground Truth entered directly by candidate.
DERIVED: Formulated from verified facts with explicit citations.
UNKNOWN: Not in vault. Zero guessing allowed; flagged for review.

2. Cryptographic Gate Envelope

GATE-8821

The backend blocks any submission without a valid, unexpired token. SHA-256 payload integrity guarantees zero unauthorized modifications.
